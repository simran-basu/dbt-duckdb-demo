Therapy Area Summary: Cardiology

Cardiology remains one of the largest and most competitive therapy areas in pharmaceutical commercial operations, driven by high disease prevalence and chronic, long-term treatment needs.

Key Disease States:
- Heart Failure (HFrEF and HFpEF): Growing focus on ARNI and SGLT2 inhibitor combination therapy.
- Hypertension: Largely genericized market with high patient volume but low per-patient revenue.
- Atrial Fibrillation: Anticoagulant market increasingly dominated by DOACs (direct oral anticoagulants) over warfarin.
- Hyperlipidemia: Statins remain first-line; PCSK9 inhibitors used for high-risk or statin-intolerant patients.

Commercial Considerations:
Field engagement in cardiology typically targets high-prescribing cardiologists and primary care physicians managing chronic populations. Formulary access and step-therapy requirements significantly influence prescribing behavior, making payer coverage data a critical input for targeting models.

Competitive Landscape:
The heart failure market has seen increased competition following the entry of multiple ARNI and SGLT2 inhibitor combination products, compressing market share for first-generation therapies.
